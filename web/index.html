PSRemotePlayTogether

Experience a new way of playing together online.

Got a link from a friend?

Paste it here. A link looks like https://…/#AbCd…; an invite from the app (psrpt://…) works too.

  1. 1Your friend shares a game with the PSRemotePlayTogether app, on their PlayStation, PC or Xbox, and sends you a link.
  2. 2You open it here, plug in a controller and join. Nothing to install, no account.
  3. 3You get the same picture and sound, and your controller plays along.
