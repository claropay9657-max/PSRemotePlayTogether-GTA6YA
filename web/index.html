PSRemotePlayTogether

Play one PlayStation together, right in your browser.

Got a link from a friend?

Paste it here. A link looks like https://…/#AbCd…; an invite from the app (psrpt://…) works too.

1Your friend shares their PlayStation with the PSRemotePlayTogether app and sends you a link.
2You open it here, plug in a controller and join. Nothing to install, no account.
3You get the same picture and sound, and your controller plays along as player 1.

You are invited to play

You get your friend's game picture and sound, and your controller plays along on their console.

Press any button on your controller so that the browser sees it.
