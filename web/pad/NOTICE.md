# Controller pictures

The pictures in this folder are the PS5 skins of **CryptoPad** by Crypto90,
https://github.com/Crypto90/CryptoPad, MIT licence: the same ones the app uses
for the display on the game picture and for the OBS overlay
(`gui/res/overlay/cryptopad`). The recoloured skins bring their own body and
face buttons and borrow everything else from the white one (`PS5`).
